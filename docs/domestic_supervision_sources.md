## Product interpretation and limits

The Korean principles in the corpus explicitly distinguish source findings from product applications. Ethical sources establish constraints; research suggests useful supervision topics. Neither establishes the truth of an individual client's formulation. The current transcript and counselor memo remain the evidence for case observations. Findings about supervisor–trainee relationships do not establish this client's relationship dynamics.

The concise narrative, uncertainty markers, counterevidence, and questions are product design choices, not an association's required summary format. The psychodynamic/relational lens remains a user-selected project choice, rather than a uniquely Korean or universally required approach. Domestic publication does not imply that a theory originated in Korea or lacks international influences.

문화는 이 내담자의 실제 경험과 조건으로 확인한다. 국적만으로 위계·체면·가족 의무를 채워 넣지 않는다. 상담자가 느낀 마음은 직접 기록된 경우에만 다루며, 하고 싶었던 반응과 실행한 개입은 구분한다. 회기의 반대 근거와 아직 남은 어려움을 지우지 않는다.

Bibliographic metadata and exact case evidence remain review data rather than repeated counselor-facing prose. No association endorsement, automated clinical approval, supervision qualification, or clinical outcome improvement is claimed.

## Maintenance

Keep each source's identity, URL, locator, principle and limitations together. Recheck official pages before changing version claims. A counselor-supplied institutional recording guide or agreed formulation reference can replace current product choices after authorization to use it. Do not ingest unconsented case records as general reference material.
