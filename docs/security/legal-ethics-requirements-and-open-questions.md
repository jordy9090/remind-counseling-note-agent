## Retention and institutional decisions

The official association code E1 §5(b) references institutional retention rules for public/educational institutions and a principle of retention within three years otherwise. It also requires record handling rules. This is an association-specific ethical standard, not a universal Re:mind default, a rule for all associations, or a conclusion about statutory retention duties.

For participating voucher providers, V1 printed p. 82 identifies five-year preservation for specified service-contract, service/cost-claim, and user-payment records. It does not turn every temporary recording, model request, embedding or private reflection into a five-year record. V1 p. 102 requires a service record including user name/date of birth, provider name, service content/date, pre/post psychological test results and user signature, with stated signature alternatives; p. 136 contains Form 10. Q149 on p. 204 distinguishes recommended standard-manual session/termination forms from mandatory program records. Confirm the applicable customer and any updates before implementing that mapping.

No deletion control should silently resolve a conflict between an original-input preference and a protected final record. The decision packet must name artifact, purpose, custodian, legal/contractual source, retention trigger, period, deletion/hold behavior, derivatives, backups and responsible approver. Policy choices remain gated by founder approval.

## Open decisions and evidence boundaries

1. **Before real counseling data:** establish customer authority, sensitive-data basis, recording/third-party handling, processor contracts, overseas processing and the actual provider settings. No real case materials were inspected for this work.
2. **Before new public claims:** substantiate the exact operator-access and deletion guarantees. RLS isolation and encryption in transit/at rest do not prove end-to-end encryption or that platform operators cannot read data.
3. **Before report-policy changes:** approve readers and fields in the audience matrix; resolve client access requests and institutional submission duties with the record custodian and experts. A counselor-private classification is not a determination that a client has no legal access right.
4. **Before retention changes:** approve the per-artifact schedule and migration/rollback plan. Remote DB/RLS changes, existing-record deletion, Production deployment and merging to main remain expressly approval-gated.
5. **Still unverified:** current association AI guidance; live provider region/retention/contracts; hosted RLS; customer-specific voucher/institution rules; full AI-notice coverage; any high-impact AI or medical-product classification.
